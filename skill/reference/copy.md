# Copy

Write like a person who uses the product. Not a marketer.

## Banned Words

"Elevate", "Unlock", "Empower", "Seamless", "Robust", "Delve", "Pivotal", "Tapestry", "In today's fast-paced world", "Whether you're a... or a...", "Let's dive in", "In summary".

## Banned Punctuation

- Em dashes as decoration
- Straight apostrophes in prose (use the curly right one)
- Straight quotes in prose
- Ellipsis as suspense

## Labels

Buttons name their action:
- "Save draft" not "Submit"
- "Delete forever" not "Confirm"
- "Send invite" not "Continue"

## Errors

Name the problem AND the recovery:
- "Email is missing an @. Try again." not "Invalid input"
- "We couldn't reach the server. Check your connection." not "Error occurred"
- "This file is 12 MB. Limit is 5 MB." not "Upload failed"

## Empty States

Teach the interface. Not "No items yet."

Good: "No projects yet. Create one to get started."
Good: "No results for 'blue widget'. Try a shorter search."
Good: "You're offline. Changes will sync when you reconnect."

## Confirmation

Say what happened, briefly.

- "Saved." not "Your changes have been successfully saved."
- "Deleted." not "The item has been deleted from your account."

## Loading

Name the real operation:
- "Loading your projects..." not "Loading..."
- "Saving changes..." not "Please wait"

## Voice

Direct, specific, rooted in craft.
No hedging. No "you might consider".
Say what to do. Say what happened. Say what to do next.
