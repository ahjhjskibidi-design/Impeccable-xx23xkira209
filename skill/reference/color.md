# Color

Color is hierarchy, meaning, and atmosphere. Not decoration.

## Strategy

Pick ONE:

- Restrained: neutrals plus one accent. Most UI.
- Committed: one saturated color carries 30-60% of the surface.
- Full palette: 3-4 named roles.
- Drenched: the surface IS the color.

## Neutrals

Never pure black or pure white.

- Off-white: #f7f4ed, #f5f3ee, #faf7f2
- Dark: #12110f, #0d0e0c, #17161a

Tint neutrals toward the hue family for cohesion. Warm subject: warm grays. Cool subject: cool grays.

## Contrast

- Body text: 4.5:1 or higher
- Large text: 3:1 or higher
- Controls, icons, focus indicators: 3:1 or higher

Check with a tool. Never eyeball.

## Color on Color

Never gray text on a colored background. Derive the secondary text from the surface hue or the foreground. Gray on color is the single most common legibility failure.

## The One Accent Rule

Accent used for primary action, current selection, and state indicator. Not decoration. Its rarity is the point.

## Semantic Colors

- Success: not always green
- Warning: not always yellow
- Error: not always red

Follow the platform and domain convention unless the world overrides it.

## Dark Mode

Design dark explicitly. Do not invert the light theme.

- Surfaces get darker, not lighter
- Shadows become glows where appropriate
- Saturated colors need desaturation
- Contrast target stays the same
